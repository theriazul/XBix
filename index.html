ABOUT THIS TOOL

Useful information, without the overclaim.

NumberCheck validates number structure against international numbering metadata. A valid format does not prove the number is assigned, active, reachable, or owned by a particular person.

Privacy & limitations

Phone checks run in your browser. Recent numbers use this browser's local storage until you clear them. Place names go to Photon for geographic search; GPS coordinates are not sent to that search service or saved in history. Maps and data load from third parties when online.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Privacy & Limitations

A clear guide to what this browser-based tool can access, and what it cannot.

STEP 01

Data privacy

In its current frontend-only implementation, this website does not automatically collect or upload precise GPS coordinates to a server. When you choose to share your device location, coordinates are held in this page only and are not sent to a server by the app.

STEP 02

Phone number information

A supported international number can be checked against numbering-plan metadata to identify its country calling code and region. A phone number alone cannot reveal its owner's identity, home address, current district, or live GPS location.

STEP 03

GPS location permission

Device location is requested only after you explicitly click Share my location, and only if you grant the browser's location permission. You can clear the displayed coordinates at any time.

STEP 04

Location accuracy

Reported GPS accuracy depends on your device, browser, available location signals, and surroundings. A displayed coordinate and its accuracy estimate may not identify an exact address.

STEP 05

Map services

When you are online, map tiles and other resources are requested from third-party services, including OpenStreetMap, Photon place search, jsDelivr country data, public CDNs, and Google Fonts. Place searches send the place-name query and selected ISO country code (if any) to Photon; phone numbers and GPS coordinates are not part of those searches. Map attribution: tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors; map interface by Leaflet. If the map is zoomed to shared GPS, tile requests can reveal the approximate area being viewed, but the app does not send raw coordinates to the tile service. If you open the coordinate link in OpenStreetMap, coordinates are included in that URL request.

STEP 06

Local search history

Up to five successfully validated phone numbers are saved in this browser's local storage to show recent checks. They remain until replaced or you choose Clear history. GPS coordinates are not added to search history. This browser storage is not encrypted, and scripts running in this page can technically access it; clear history on shared devices.

STEP 07

Important limitations

This website is not connected to private telecom databases. It cannot identify subscribers, retrieve private registration details, or secretly track another person's phone or GPS location. A country-level map is not a phone's live position.

No phone tracing. No hidden location access.
